<objective>
Close the confirmed live deadlock: a service restart destroys the in-memory session timer
while `booth_state` stays `BUSY` in SQLite, so the booth never recovers and every guest
behind it is unreachable. This plan adds the persisted-state-anchored startup reconciliation
(D-01..D-04), the bounded retry policy that a stalled job goes through (D-05..D-07), the two
storage methods that policy needs, and the typed-config and real-logger fixes the rest of the
phase depends on (D-13, M2 success criterion 5).

Purpose: as of this writing the live database has read `booth_state = BUSY` since
`2026-08-10T04:29:42.732Z` — over 60x the 5-minute `SESSION_TIMEOUT_MS` — with four
`queue_job` rows stranded in `dispatched` and one guest (`job-webhook-001`) queued behind
them. Three of those four rows date from 2026-08-06, an earlier incident, and are invisible
to any recovery that reads only the single `booth_state` row. This is the phase's centre of
gravity.

Output: `LocalStore.getInFlightJobs()`, `LocalStore.requeueWithRetry()`, `LocalStore.failJob()`,
`QueueService.recover()`, `QueueService.handleStall()`, two new `EnvConfig` keys, a queue
logger wired into Winston, one line of `index.ts` wiring, and an integration test that
reproduces the live four-row scenario against a real temp SQLite file.
