<objective>
**As a** booth operator, **I want to** start and configure the Agent from a window on the booth PC,
**so that** running a photo booth never depends on remembering a terminal command.
*(Phase story; this plan is the slice that removes the retyped token and the accidental shutdown.)*

Turn the tracer window into the operator surface the phase promised: a first run that imports the
`.env` already sitting on the booth PC, a settings form that explains its refusals, a status panel
that answers the three questions an operator asks, a live log pane, a tray the application minimizes
to instead of dying, and a start-with-Windows checkbox.

Purpose: success criterion 3 in full, and the half of criteria 1 and 2 that is about a person rather
than a process — the friction this phase exists to remove is retyping a token at a booth and losing
the relay because somebody clicked the close button. Everything here is provable headless except
what only a real Windows desktop can show; that half goes to the gate in plan 10-04, named.

Output: the `.env` importer, the log tail with its masking and its bounds, the tray and login-item
integration, the preferences the two of them need, the finished renderer, and three test files.
</objective>
