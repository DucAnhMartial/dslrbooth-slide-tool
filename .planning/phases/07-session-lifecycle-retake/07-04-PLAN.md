<objective>
Give a session that truly ended with no photo its own visible outcome — distinct
from a retake, distinct from a normal session, and distinct from a stall.

Purpose: today such a session is indistinguishable from a successful one. ROADMAP
success criterion 3 is only achievable if the outcome is a persisted fact rather
than an inference, and D-09 chose a sixth internal job status over a reason column
for two properties: forgetting to map it is a compile error, and the wait-estimate
sample query excludes it by construction rather than by a filter someone could
delete.

Output: `'no_photo'` as the sixth member of `KNOWN_JOB_STATUSES` with its
compile-enforced mapping, a `closeJobNoPhoto()` writer that charges no retry, the
`sessionEnded()` branch that calls it, and the guest-facing translation to
`see_staff` with `photoDelivery: 'failed'`.
</objective>
