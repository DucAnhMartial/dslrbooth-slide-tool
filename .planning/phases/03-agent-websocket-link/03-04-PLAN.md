<objective>
Make a dead Agent cost the guest their seat for seconds rather than five minutes — and cost them
nothing else — and stop seven of AGT-04's ten event types from falling through with no handler at
all.

Purpose: today `ws.on('close')` only nulls the local connection object (`agentServer.ts:80-85`).
If the booth is BUSY with a dispatched job, nothing tells the queue. It is not a deadlock —
Phase 2's five-minute `busyTimer` eventually fires — but the guest waits out the full timeout in
front of a booth the server already knows is dead, and when the timer does fire it charges them a
retry for a fault that was entirely the booth's. D-01 forbids that, and D-12 specifies the fix.

`handleStall()` cannot be reused for it. That method is the single definition of Phase 2's
D-05/D-06/D-07 policy and **always** increments `retries` (`queueService.ts:122-130`,
`localStore.ts:206-212`). Bending it to sometimes skip the increment would make its one job
ambiguous and put Phase 2's verified guarantee at risk. A sibling that is small, parallel and
obviously different at the call site is the safer shape — and it makes the D-01 contrast legible
in the code: session-side faults charge, system-side faults do not.

Second: `onAgentEvent()` (`queueService.ts:170-178`) is an `if/else if` chain with no final
`else`. `session_end`, `session_start` and `file_upload` are handled; `countdown_start`,
`countdown`, `capture_start`, `file_download`, `processing_start`, `sharing_screen` and
`printing` are silently dropped. AGT-04 says these events are "received and processed" — D-14
requires this plan to say what "processed" means for each rather than treating three as
sufficient.

Output: `LocalStore.requeueNoRetry()`; `QueueService.onAgentDisconnected()`; the wiring line that
binds them to plan 03-02's transport seam; a final `else` in the event chain; and two new test
files, one of which proves the no-retry guarantee against real SQL rather than a mock.
