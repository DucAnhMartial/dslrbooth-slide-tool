copy-suffix composite, the three-door idempotency, and the "ignored" lines themselves.

misfire. The gallery reprint happened in production and was silent. The auto-print setting is a
one-checkbox operational precondition with no runtime enforcement — the only thing that can make it
visible is a test that shows what breaks without it.
