<objective>
Give the operator the third finalize door: a "end this guest" control on the CMS, shown only when
there is a guest to end.

D11-08 exists because the one piece of information no trigger carries is "the guest walked away".
Only a human standing in the room has it. The grace timer is the safety net; this button is the
fast path, and it is what keeps the 20 s window from becoming the normal cost of a walk-away when
the room is busy.

This is also the first queue MUTATION any CMS route in this codebase has ever been able to reach.
Every existing CMS interface is a deliberately narrow read-mostly view over the store, and
`cmsEventRoutes.ts` says so in its own header. The narrowness is preserved here by construction:
the route gets one read and one write, both on `QueueService`, and no store handle at all.

Purpose: close SC-4 and give scenario 4 of the live booth gate something to exercise.
Output: `GET /cms/booth`, `POST /cms/api/booth/release`, the narrow link interface, the wiring,
and the route test.
</objective>
