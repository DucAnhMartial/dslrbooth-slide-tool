<objective>
Write down what the operator has to know and then prove it at the booth.

Two of this phase's guarantees do not live in code at all. The first is auto-print being OFF: it is
a checkbox in dslrBooth on the booth PC, nothing in this repository can enforce it, and every other
guarantee in this phase rests on it. The second is that a guest who complains is answered by
reading one summary line and two log tokens — which only works if those are published.

Then the phase's last success criterion: the five measured scenarios, run by a human, on the real
booth, with a second guest genuinely queued. Phase 7 stopped at the same kind of gate and stopping
there was correct. This one is the same shape.

Purpose: close SC-6, and make SC-4 and SC-5 legible to someone who was not in this phase.
Output: DEPLOYMENT.md's precondition and configuration entries, a new OPS-RUNBOOK section plus
three amended ones, the validation map marked complete, and a recorded gate result.

    The full attempt-and-approval model is shipped and green in the suite: a retake keeps the guest's
    session and their last photo, an unapproved walk-away is bounded by the grace window, a gallery
    reprint finalizes nothing, the operator can end a guest from the CMS, and the composite is found

  <how-to-verify>
    Before starting: confirm auto-print is OFF in dslrBooth on the booth PC, confirm the service is
    running the build from this phase, and confirm no job is left queued. Have a second guest — a
    real person or a second registration — genuinely queued for scenarios 1 and 2.

    1. **Retake with a guest queued.** Guest A shoots, presses Retake on the sharing screen, shoots
       again, presses print. Expected: A receives exactly ONE notification and its photo is the
       second attempt's; guest B is not announced or dispatched at any moment before A's print; the
       summary line for A reads outcome completed, captures 2, attempts 2, approved_by printing.
    2. **Walk-away.** Guest A shoots and leaves without pressing print. Expected: after the grace
       window B is called; A's summary line reads approved_by grace and A receives the last attempt's
       photo, or is closed without a photo if none arrived.
    3. **Gallery reprint during another guest's session.** While B is shooting, reprint A's photo
       from the dslrBooth gallery. Expected: a log line saying the composite is not this attempt's;
       B is not finalized and B's own session completes normally afterwards.
    4. **Operator end.** Guest A shoots, does not print. Open the CMS booth page, confirm it shows A
       with an elapsed time, press "end this guest". Expected: immediate release, and A's summary
       line reads approved_by operator.
    5. **Both layouts.** Run one session on the 4-shot layout and one on the 1-shot layout. Expected:
       no warning about an unusable composite path in either.

    Record for each scenario: what happened, the summary line, and any unexpected log line. A
    scenario that behaves differently from the expectation is a result to report, not a failure to
    work around at the booth.
  </how-to-verify>
