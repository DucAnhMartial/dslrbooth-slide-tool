<objective>
Give the persisted retry queue its failure posture: bound every attempt in wall-clock time, record *why* an attempt failed in terms an operator can act on, stop retrying at a stated budget, and guarantee that no single bad row can take down the sweep.

Plan 04-01 proved the delivery path end to end on the happy path. This plan hardens the unhappy paths on the same code, and is where NOT-02 ("retry giới hạn khi gửi thất bại — log để xử lý thủ công") is actually earned.

Purpose: the current `fetch` has no timeout at all, so a receiver that accepts a connection and never answers holds an attempt open indefinitely — and because the call site does not await, nothing anywhere notices (D-07). Retry counting also disagreed with Phase 2's until 04-01 flipped it; this plan is where that flip is *proved* rather than asserted (D-08).

Output: a delivery path that fails fast, fails legibly, fails a bounded number of times, and fails in isolation.
